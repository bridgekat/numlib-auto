# Temporary notes

During a formalization project, the orchestrator agent may give further detailed instructions to sub-agents through additional notes. The sub-agents may also want to store their learned lessons which the orchestrator should summarize and record. This directory is for all such notes.

To avoid context blowup, the orchestrator should periodically summarize or clean up less relevant notes in this directory.

`qss/` holds the second planning round (Quarteroni–Sacco–Saleri, 2026-09): the brief the chapter sub-agents worked from, and one directory per chapter with the sub-agent's report (inventory of every numbered item with its verdict, errata, open questions) and its Lean prototypes, which the plan descriptions cite by path. `notes/backbone.md` §17 is the consolidated record; the reports are the raw one and can be pruned as the surface is written.
